# Basement on Roblox (Mac, free)

No CrossOver. No paid Windows layer. Roblox Studio + a free Roblox account.

## Why this path

- Free on Mac  
- Built-in multiplayer + custom avatars  
- Looks like an actual 3D game, not the crude browser sketch  
- **Auto-built** — you paste one script and press Play (no hours of hand-placing)

## Checklist

### A. Account
- [ ] Free account at [roblox.com](https://www.roblox.com)
- [ ] Install **Roblox** + **Roblox Studio**

### B. Auto-build (≈2 min)
Follow **[AUTOBUILD.md](AUTOBUILD.md)** — paste `BuildBasement.server.lua` into a Script under ServerScriptService → Play once → Publish.

### C. Play with friends
- [ ] Place settings: friends can join
- [ ] Share the Roblox place link (Bitly optional)

Manual part-by-part steps in `STUDIO-STEP-1.md` are **obsolete** — use the auto-build script instead.
